K.I.S.S. Guide

KNYWBot, the short version

Keep It Simple, Stupid. Everything you actually need to know to use the bot — nothing else.

💬

Talk to it

Tag @KNYWBot with anything — a question, a build request, nonsense — in #knywbot-testing or #💀-town-square.

🎬

It might drop a GIF

No action needed — every so often it reacts to a good moment in chat on its own.

⭐

Give karma

Type @user++ to give, @user-- to take. Check totals with /karma.

🚀

Need a boost?

Run /request-boost, or just tell the bot — @KNYWBot I need a boost to lvl 70 — and it posts it for you.

🧭

Need a group?

Run /request-group, or ask the bot — @KNYWBot find me a group for helltides.

🏷️

Share your BattleTag

Set it once with /battletag-set, then show it off anytime with /bt.

One more thing

Don't

Spam, drop Discord invite links, or bring up cheats/gold-selling/account-selling — nothing auto-deletes it, but a mod can drop you a tracked warning with /warn.
